Frequently Asked ...

These are frequently asked questions on Tech MasterChef

Who can participate?

Anyone passionate about development, design, UI/UX, or AI, and eager to learn, explore new ideas, and enhance their skills is welcome to participate.

What is inside the boxes?

Hints pointing to a real-life problem, plus the ingredients (constraints, target users, features) your solution must use. No peeking before the opening ceremony.

How many participants are allowed in a team?

Teams of 4 people. Solo chefs are welcome too we'll help you find a team on the spot.

Do I need prior knowledge in coding or design?

No need to master everything: teams mix developers, designers, UI/UX and AI profiles so skills complement each other, and mentors visit the teams to guide them.
